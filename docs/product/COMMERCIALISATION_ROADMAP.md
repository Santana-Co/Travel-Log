# Travel Log Commercialisation Roadmap

## Strategy

Follow an evidence-led, web-first progression:

**Build → Refine → Validate → Commercialise → Scale → Mobile → Expand**

Travel Log is positioned as an Australian work-travel, compliance, and evidence platform. Native mobile is a later expansion, not a prerequisite for validating the product.

## Build

Complete the dependable web core: accurate records and dates, explicit classification, reliable distance handling, financial-year reporting, versioned Australian rules, audit evidence, useful exports, and safe account/data controls.

The current pilot already provides trip CRUD, saved locations, protected route calculation, multiple recording methods, ATO estimates/logbook workflows, and exports. Classification, audit evidence, reusable vehicles, and stronger financial-year reporting remain gaps.

## Refine

Reduce friction and build trust through realistic browser/device testing, accessibility work, onboarding, error recovery, performance measurement, privacy review, report reconciliation, and production observability. Critical security or data-integrity problems block release regardless of feature completeness.

## Validate

Interview and observe field-based Australian workers and sole traders, then run a closed web pilot. Measure signup-to-first-trip activation, repeated recording, report/export use, distance failures, support effort, retention, and willingness to pay. Use synthetic or minimised telemetry and avoid collecting unnecessary location history.

Suggested pilot sizes, conversion targets, prices, and customer demand are commercial hypotheses. Set them only as experiments and revise them from evidence.

## Commercialise

Introduce a paid web beta only after the core workflow is trusted and supportable. Validate packaging before building complex entitlement logic. Possible packages include an individual record-keeping tier, a richer professional/evidence tier, and later business workflows.

Commercial readiness also requires billing lifecycle safety, appropriate legal review, support operations, monitoring, incident response, backups/recovery, export/deletion, and a repeatable release process.

## Scale

Test efficient Australian acquisition channels—accountants/bookkeepers, professional communities, field-service and care audiences, educational content, small-business partnerships, and product referrals—only after retention is credible. Improve automation where it measurably reduces recording effort while preserving confirmation and control.

## Mobile

Build native clients only when validated demand requires native capabilities and shared backend/rules contracts are stable. Likely value includes offline capture, push reminders, and carefully designed trip-detection assistance.

## Expand

Employer reimbursement, submissions/approvals, policy enforcement, payroll/accounting exports, organisation administration, accountant workflows, integrations, APIs, and new jurisdictions are potential B2B or platform expansions. They are not implemented or validated commitments.

## Review cadence

- Monthly: workflow evidence, usage, incidents, support themes, retention, commercial experiments, roadmap stage, and debt.
- Quarterly: architecture, privacy/security, recovery, dependencies, performance, observability, unused complexity, and milestone blockers.

Health reviews produce prioritised recommendations; they do not authorise automatic feature or infrastructure work.
