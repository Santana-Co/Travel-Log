# Travel Log Product Vision

## Mission

Build a trusted Australian work-travel, compliance, and evidence platform that makes recording, explaining, reporting, and eventually reimbursing work-related travel simple.

Travel Log should grow beyond a mileage tracker into a dependable system of record for individuals and, once the individual workflow is validated, organisations with mobile workforces.

## Problem

People who travel for work often reconstruct journeys from memory, calendars, odometer notes, or spreadsheets. That creates missed records, weak evidence, inconsistent reimbursement claims, and tax-time administration. Travel Log should reduce that burden while keeping the user in control of sensitive location data.

## Initial audiences

Likely early users include field-based Australian employees, sole traders, care and support workers, IT technicians, consultants, sales representatives, trades, and small field-service businesses. These are target hypotheses; demand and willingness to pay have not yet been validated.

## Product promise

A user should be able to answer:

- Where, when, and why did I travel?
- How far did I travel, and how was that distance established?
- Which records are relevant to work, reimbursement, or an Australian income year?
- What evidence can I provide to an accountant or employer?
- Which rule and assumption produced an estimate?

## Principles

- **Trust before feature count:** correctness, data durability, privacy, and tenant isolation are foundational.
- **Web first:** make the responsive web application robust, secure, polished, and commercially viable before building native clients.
- **Explainable rules:** time-sensitive Australian rules need effective dates, versions, sources, and boundary tests.
- **Evidence matters:** material corrections should ultimately retain actor, time, reason, and before/after evidence.
- **User control:** automation should suggest and assist, not silently classify or alter records.
- **Simple common path:** record, review, and export should remain fast.

## Product directions

- **Individual:** work-travel records, estimates, evidence, and reports.
- **Pro:** richer reporting, vehicles, audit evidence, automation, and accountant workflows.
- **Business:** organisations, employees, policies, approvals, reimbursement, and payroll/accounting exports.

These are strategic directions, not implemented plans or validated packages. The current product is an authenticated individual-use pilot.

## Commercial success

Success means users repeatedly record travel, trust the resulting evidence and reports, and find the time saved valuable enough to pay for. Pricing, conversion, retention, segment demand, and channel performance remain hypotheses until measured with real users.
