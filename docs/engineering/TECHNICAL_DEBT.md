## Maintenance rules

- Add evidence when changing status; never mark a debt resolved from intention alone.
- Keep unresolved residual risk as a separate active item rather than overstating a partial resolution.
- Reassess priorities before stage exits and material releases.
- Product ideas belong in the roadmap unless they describe a concrete current-system liability.
